LevelChinese News **v0.7.6** is out for Android. The big addition is **Sentence examples** under the Learn tab: type a Chinese word and see real sentences from our news corpus. Tap a result to open the full article and keep reading in context.

Sentence audio is now generally available for all articles — tap the speaker icon beside any sentence to hear it read aloud. [Download the Android v0.7.6 APK](https://expo.dev/artifacts/eas/DdpNswTXyOOo2bYRtR4DMiG5U-YSGqJkES6ykV_Kr-w.apk).

This release includes some style and copy fixes for the dark mode and parse article url screen.
