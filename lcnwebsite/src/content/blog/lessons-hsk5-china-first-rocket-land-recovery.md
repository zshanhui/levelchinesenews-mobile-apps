## Industry terms and phrases to know

| Term  | Pinyin            | English                                                         |
| ----- | ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 朱雀三号  | Zhūquè Sānhào     | Zhuque-3 (LandSpace reusable launch vehicle)                    |
| 运载火箭  | yùnzài huǒjiàn    | launch vehicle; carrier rocket                                  |
| 一子级   | yī zǐ jí          | first stage (of a rocket)                                       |
| 着陆腿   | zhuólù tuǐ        | landing legs                                                    |
| 陆地回收  | lùdì huíshōu      | land recovery (recovering a rocket on the ground)               |
| 可重复使用 | kě chóngfù shǐyòng | reusable                                                        |
| 液氧甲烷  | yèyǎng jiǎwán     | liquid oxygen and methane (rocket propellant)                     |
| 可控回收  | kěkòng huíshōu    | controlled recovery                                             |
| 网系回收  | wǎng xì huíshōu   | net-based recovery (catching a booster in a net)                  |

2026 年 8 月 19 日早上 7 时 35 分，中国公司蓝箭航天<mark class="vocab">发射</mark>了一枚叫<mark class="vocab">朱雀三号</mark>的<mark class="vocab">运载火箭</mark>。这是它的第二次飞行，新闻里写作「遥二」。火箭<mark class="vocab">一子级</mark>按预定程序回到地面，成功完成<mark class="vocab">陆地回收</mark>。这是中国首次用<mark class="vocab">着陆腿</mark>回收<mark class="vocab">可重复使用</mark>火箭的一子级。官方说，这是一项重大<mark class="vocab">突破</mark>。

<details class="translation">
<summary>English translation</summary>
<p>At 7:35 a.m. on August 19, 2026, the Chinese company LandSpace launched a launch vehicle called Zhuque-3. This was its second flight (news reports write "Yao-2"). The rocket's first stage returned to the ground on schedule and completed a land recovery. This was the first time China used landing legs to recover a reusable rocket's first stage. Officials called it a major breakthrough.</p>
</details>

在此之前，中国已经在海上做过火箭<mark class="vocab">回收</mark>试验。今年 7 月 11 日，中国第一次在海上完成<mark class="vocab">可控回收</mark>，并用了「<mark class="vocab">网系回收</mark>」技术。不过，这次陆地回收仍然很重要。报道说，可重复使用火箭的<mark class="vocab">难点</mark>不只是「上得去」，更在于「回得来、落得准」。

<details class="translation">
<summary>English translation</summary>
<p>Before this, China had already tested rocket recovery at sea. On July 11 this year, China completed its first controlled recovery at sea, using "net-based recovery." Still, this land recovery was important. Reports say the hard part of reusable rockets is not just getting up, but coming back and landing accurately.</p>
</details>

这次发射原本定在 8 月 11 日。8 月 10 日，海南文昌<mark class="vocab">发射场</mark>有另一枚火箭发射失败，朱雀三号因此<mark class="vocab">推迟</mark>到 8 月 19 日。

<details class="translation">
<summary>English translation</summary>
<p>This launch had originally been set for August 11. On August 10, another rocket failed at the Wenchang launch site in Hainan, so Zhuque-3 was postponed to August 19.</p>
</details>

朱雀三号是一枚低成本、可重复使用的新火箭，<mark class="vocab">燃料</mark>是<mark class="vocab">液氧甲烷</mark>。它全长 66.1 米，起飞重量 570 吨。负责人说，这种燃料一般只占发射成本的 1% 到 3%，而一子级成本约占整枚火箭的 70%。所以，重复使用的次数越多，单次发射成本就越低。

<details class="translation">
<summary>English translation</summary>
<p>Zhuque-3 is a new low-cost, reusable rocket. Its fuel is liquid oxygen and methane. It is 66.1 meters long and weighs 570 tons at liftoff. A project leader said this fuel usually accounts for only 1% to 3% of launch cost, while the first stage makes up about 70% of the whole rocket. So the more times it is reused, the lower the cost per launch.</p>
</details>

路透社报道，蓝箭航天是继马斯克的 SpaceX 和贝索斯的蓝色起源（Blue Origin）之后，第三家做到火箭陆地回收的<mark class="vocab">民营企业</mark>。分析人士认为，这次成功缩小了中美在可重复使用火箭上的<mark class="vocab">差距</mark>。

<details class="translation">
<summary>English translation</summary>
<p>Reuters reported that LandSpace is the third private company to land-recover a rocket, after Elon Musk's SpaceX and Jeff Bezos's Blue Origin. Analysts say this success narrows the gap between China and the United States in reusable rockets.</p>
</details>

同一天，嫦娥七号也在准备发射，目标是<mark class="vocab">探测</mark>月球南极。

<details class="translation">
<summary>English translation</summary>
<p>On the same day, Chang'e-7 was also preparing to launch, with the goal of exploring the Moon's south pole.</p>
</details>

一句话概括：8 月 19 日，蓝箭航天的朱雀三号第一次在中国完成一子级陆地回收。和海上回收不同，这次是用着陆腿落回地面；因为一子级最贵，重复使用以后，发射成本会明显下降。

<details class="translation">
<summary>English translation</summary>
<p>In one sentence: on August 19, LandSpace's Zhuque-3 completed China's first land recovery of a first stage. Unlike recovery at sea, this time it came down on landing legs; because the first stage is the expensive part, reuse will bring launch costs down.</p>
</details>
