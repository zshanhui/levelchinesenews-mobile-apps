We are getting ready for the next phase of LearnChineseNews beta testing.

Over the last few weeks, we focused on a smoother reading workflow:

- better article parsing for long-form stories
- cleaner sentence-by-sentence translation flow
- improved local dictionary setup and reliability

The goal remains the same: make native Chinese news more approachable for intermediate learners without turning the app into a noisy platform.

Next, we are prioritizing article simplification experiments and vocabulary preview enhancements. If you are testing the app and want to share feedback, we would love to hear from you.
