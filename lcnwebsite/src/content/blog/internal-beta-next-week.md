We will begin **internal beta** testing next week with a limited group of early testers. The focus is on day-to-day stability, install and update paths, and whether the reader and dictionary workflows feel natural on real devices.

Feedback from this round will guide fixes and polish before we expand to a broader beta. If you are already on the list, you will receive install instructions and a short checklist when the build is ready.
