2026 年 6 月 6 日晚，15 岁的中国网球选手孙心然参加了法国网球公开赛（法网）青少年女单决赛。她输给了对手，获得亚军。虽然没能夺冠，但这次成绩仍然很重要。6 月 8 日公布的新排名里，她成为国际网球联合会青少年女单世界第一。球迷叫她「小蝴蝶」。很多人希望，她将来能像李娜一样，在世界网坛打出名堂。

> On the evening of June 6, 2026, 15-year-old Chinese tennis player Sun Xinran played in the French Open junior girls' final. She lost and finished as runner-up. Although she did not win the title, the result still mattered. In the new rankings released on June 8, she became world No. 1 in ITF junior girls' singles. Fans call her "Little Butterfly." Many hope she can one day make her mark on the world tennis stage, like Li Na.

打进决赛之前，孙心然已经连赢 15 场比赛。对一个年轻球员来说，连续高强度比赛很不容易，既考验体力，也考验心态。她在采访中说，比赛时她很少想「最后会赢还是输」，只想打好每一分。回顾这 15 连胜，她觉得最大的收获是心态更稳了：很多次比分落后，她也能冷静追分，最后翻盘。

> Before the final, Sun Xinran had won 15 matches in a row. For a young player, playing at that intensity is hard—it tests both fitness and mindset. In an interview, she said she rarely thinks about winning or losing during a match; she just focuses on every point. Looking back on the 15-match streak, she said the biggest gain was a steadier mindset: even when behind, she could stay calm, fight back, and sometimes complete a comeback.

决赛里，她的对手是一位比她大两岁的俄罗斯选手，身体和比赛经验都更接近成年选手。孙心然很快输掉了比赛。她说，这场失利很遗憾，但也让她看清自己的不足：力量还不够强，发球和接发球也还不够好。面对更强大的对手，她在身体素质上还有明显差距。她认为，这场比赛帮她找到了下一步该提高的方向。

> In the final, her opponent was a Russian player two years older, with a body and match experience closer to an adult pro. Sun Xinran lost quickly. She said the defeat was disappointing, but it also showed her weaknesses: she is not strong enough yet, and her serve and return still need work. Against stronger opponents, the gap in physical ability is clear. She thinks this match showed her what to improve next.

孙心然来自广东深圳。因为妈妈打网球，她从小就开始学这项运动，也很快就显示出天赋。11 岁时，她决定将来做职业网球运动员，并开始长期在国外训练和比赛。她说，这个决定主要是自己的意愿，也离不开父母支持。因为经常出国比赛，她不能像普通同学一样每天去学校，只能上网课。即使训练很累，她仍坚持每天线上学习大约一个半小时。「读书不能放弃。」她说。

> Sun Xinran is from Shenzhen, Guangdong. Because her mother played tennis, she started learning the sport young and quickly showed talent. At 11, she decided to become a professional tennis player and began training and competing abroad for long stretches. She said the decision was mainly her own choice, with her parents' support. Because she travels so much for matches, she cannot attend school like most classmates and takes online classes instead. Even when training is exhausting, she still studies online for about an hour and a half each day. "I definitely can't give up studying," she said.

今年年初，她第一次在大满贯赛事——澳大利亚网球公开赛（澳网）青少年组——感受到很大压力，比赛中情绪一度很难控制。短短几个月后，来到法网，她的表现明显更成熟、更冷静。她说，现在对比赛压力看得更开了：「只要尽全力就好。」她以李娜为偶像，也关注本届法网成年组女单冠军。她明白，顶级运动员不仅技术要好，心态也要非常强。短短几个月里，她在这方面进步很大。

> Earlier this year, at her first Grand Slam—the Australian Open juniors—she felt intense pressure and struggled to control her emotions during matches. Just a few months later, at the French Open, she was noticeably more mature and calm. She said she now takes competitive pressure more lightly: "I just need to give my all." She looks up to Li Na and also follows this year's French Open women's singles champion. She understands that top athletes need not only strong skills but also strong mental control. In just a few months, she has improved a lot in that area.

成为青少年世界第一，孙心然并不觉得代表全部。她说，青少年排名没有那么重要，青少年比赛最大的价值是锻炼。她的目标，是从青少年赛场平稳进入成人职业网坛。她知道，这一步非常难：成人比赛对身体、心智和节奏的要求都更高。她正处在转型的开始阶段，前面还有很多挑战。拿到法网亚军后，她几乎没有停留，已经前往下一个赛场。

> Becoming world No. 1 among juniors does not mean everything to Sun Xinran. She said junior rankings are not that important; the main value of junior events is practice. Her goal is to move smoothly from junior tennis into the professional adult tour. She knows that step is very difficult: adult matches demand more in body, mind, and pace. She is only at the start of that transition, with many challenges ahead. After the French Open runner-up finish, she barely paused before heading to her next tournament.

一句话概括：孙心然在法网青少组拿到亚军并登顶青少年世界第一，但她更清楚，真正难的是从青少年比赛进入职业网坛，因此她一边提高技术和身体，一边继续磨练心态。

> In one sentence: Sun Xinran finished runner-up at the French Open juniors and reached world No. 1 among juniors, but she knows the harder part is moving from junior tennis to the pro tour—so she keeps improving her skills and body while sharpening her mindset.
