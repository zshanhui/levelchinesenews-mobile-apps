#### Key Features

- Real News, Most Content
  - We only support real news sites from geniune sources and current events
- Best Chinese Reader for Learners
  - The best reader, crafted for Chinese language learners with light/dark mode, adjust font sizes and spacing
- Local Dictionary
  - built in offline first dictionary and Pleco integration support
- Pinyin and Segmentation
  - Chinese is hard, so we provide segmentation and pinyin support that can be turned on/off
- Sentence Level Translations
  - We support high quality translations for every sentence in English, Spanish, Indonesian Malay, Russian, and Arabic
- Place Bookmarks
  - Bookmark the exact sentence so you can come back to exactly where you left off in long articles
- Built to be Offline and Privacy First!
  - We built the app to be like a tool, not a platform. That means articles and most features will work offline and your data is stored on your device, not our servers.
- Never Ads, Aslways Free and Open
  - The app will never have ads, and 95% of features will always be free. We are also opensourced!

## About Page /about

Hi, thanks for your interested in our little project. I started learning Chinese in 2021 and picking up the first couple hundred vocab words was easy, I just found a frequency list and learn the first couple hundres words. I am a heritage Chinese so I always had some exposure to the spoken Chinese language, but was never able to read Chinese since literacy take active study. I decided to learn to read Chinese at a native level and to do it within 5 years, but the issue was that after the most basic levels, there is a real gap between native level books and articles and what intermediate (HSK4/5) are capable of reading without looking up every other sentence. So I decided to build LevelChineseNews to bridge the gap between intermediate reading level and native level content around Chinese New Years 2026. I built a simple version with just pinyin and segmentation, added on a basic CEDICT and added Pleco integration. What I realised is that some simple tools made even complex native level articles approachable. Eventually, I added on other tools like sentence level translations and AI assisted learning tools, more media sources so that I could always find the content I am already interested in reading.

Now, we are getting ready for internal beta testing with a few dozen users and if you are an intermediate Chinese learners, and looking to try some new tools for learning Chinese while reading real, native content, then I would like your feedback to decide what features and development path we should take next!
